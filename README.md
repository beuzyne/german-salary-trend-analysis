# Historical Gross Monthly Earnings Trend in Germany (1991-2024)

A comprehensive data cleaning, analysis, and visualization project utilizing official macroeconomic data from the German Federal Statistical Office (Destatis).

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the historical development of gross monthly earnings for full-time employees in Germany over a 33-year period (1991-2024). The goal is to evaluate long-term economic stability and investigate the historical trajectory of the gender pay gap in the region.

## Tech Stack and Skills Demonstrated
* Python: Core programming language
* Pandas: Data loading, shape inspection, and strict null-value validation (.dropna(), .isnull())
* Matplotlib: Advanced data visualization, custom grid configuration, styling, and dual-line plotting

## Analytical Process and Methodology
1. Data Sourcing: Acquired authentic public tables directly from Destatis (Statistisches Bundesamt).
2. Quality Inspection: Validated the dataset dimensions (34 rows, 4 columns) and performed structured integrity checks. 
3. Null-Value Auditing: Run structural audits returning 0 missing values, validating the pristine quality of the state dataset.
4. Visual Plotting: Generated a customized time-series line chart comparing demographic trends.

## Key Insights and Conclusions
* Consistent Economic Growth: Gross monthly earnings in Germany have shown a powerful upward trajectory. The average male earnings jumped from EUR 2,010 in 1991 to EUR 4,909 in 2024, demonstrating strong wage development.
* The Gender Pay Gap Reality: The visualization clearly illustrates a persistent wage disparity. As of 2024, men averaged EUR 4,909/month while women averaged EUR 4,256/month, indicating a net structural gap of EUR 653/month.
* Career Feasibility: With the national baseline average for general full-time employees reaching EUR 4,701 in 2024, targeting a high-tier tech salary of EUR 6,000+ as a Data Scientist in Germany is a highly realistic and attainable career goal.
